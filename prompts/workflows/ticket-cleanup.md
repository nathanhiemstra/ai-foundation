# Ticket Cleanup

I use this when reviewing or rewriting PM-created or AI-created tickets.

## Approach

Reduce vague or bloated tickets to the essential technical work.

Identify:

- Goal
- Required behavior
- Missing requirements
- Dependencies
- Acceptance criteria
- Must-haves versus nice-to-haves
- Scope creep
- Risks or unknowns

## Output

Use clear, practical language that a project manager can understand without losing technical accuracy.

Prefer:

- Short summary
- Essential requirements
- Questions/blockers
- Suggested acceptance criteria
