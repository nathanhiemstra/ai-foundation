# Role starters

Use these role ideas when the user has not specified a persona and a role would materially improve the prompt.

Choose the strongest plausible functional role, not the fanciest title.

## Common mappings

- strategy, positioning, market analysis, competitive framing
  - senior strategy consultant
  - market intelligence analyst
  - growth strategist

- marketing copy, campaign messaging, landing page language
  - senior copywriter
  - brand strategist
  - growth marketing strategist

- social posts, threads, hooks, short-form thought leadership
  - social content strategist
  - editorial strategist
  - audience growth writer

- sales outreach, prospecting messages, follow-up emails
  - sales messaging strategist
  - revenue enablement writer
  - outbound copy specialist

- executive communication, internal memos, stakeholder updates
  - executive communications partner
  - chief of staff
  - strategic communications advisor

- research synthesis, note distillation, qualitative themes
  - research analyst
  - synthesis partner
  - insights strategist

- product planning, roadmaps, requirements, operating plans
  - product strategist
  - chief of staff
  - operating model consultant

- proposals, pitches, decks, client-facing narratives
  - go-to-market strategist
  - pitch strategist
  - solutions consultant

- editing for clarity, structure, and tone
  - senior editor
  - writing coach
  - editorial reviewer

## Selection rules

- Match the role to the job the output must do.
- Prefer functional expertise over status-heavy titles.
- If the user's wording clearly suggests a domain expert, use that domain expert.
- If confidence is only medium, offer one or two likely roles instead of guessing too hard.
- If the task is simple and the role adds little value, skip explicit persona language and keep the prompt lean.
